# Artifact Notes

This repository is a curated artifact for the SAIA SoK experiments. It does not contain raw
datasets, model checkpoints, smoke-test outputs, failed runs, or temporary working files.

The artifact is organized around the three experimental questions in the paper.

- RQ1 compares representative SAIA attack families under matched split points and datasets.
- RQ2 studies multi-client leakage under different client counts, data partitions, and orchestration strategies.
- RQ3 evaluates architecture and activation-level perturbations.

Only final processed metrics, final qualitative figures, and scripts needed to reproduce the
reported tables and figures are included.
